# Retail Sales Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

This project performs **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on the [Online Retail Dataset (Kaggle)](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/tunguz/online-retail).  
The dataset contains transactions from a UK-based online retailer between **2010–2011**.

The objective is to analyze customer purchasing behavior, product performance, revenue trends, and time-based sales patterns to provide actionable business insights.

---

# Key Analysis

- Customer Behavior: Basket size distribution, identification of frequent and high-value customers, and validation of the Pareto principle (20% of customers contribute to ~80% of revenue).

- Product Insights: Quantity distribution, best-selling products, and product concentration analysis for inventory and promotion planning.

- Revenue Analysis: Invoice-level revenue, customer revenue patterns, and contribution by top countries.

- Time-based Insights: Monthly and weekday sales trends highlighting seasonality and shopping peaks.

- Pricing Insights: Distribution of unit prices, focus on low-margin/high-volume sales, and identification of premium product opportunities.

- Correlation Analysis: Relationship between quantity, revenue, and price, showing that revenue is primarily volume-driven.

---

# Business Recommendations

- Strengthen engagement with top customers and focus on high-performing products to maximize ROI.

- Prepare seasonal campaigns and inventory ramp-ups ahead of November and December.

- Introduce bulk-purchase discounts to encourage larger orders.

- Explore growth opportunities in international markets beyond the UK.

- Maintain consistent stock availability for the best-selling items.
